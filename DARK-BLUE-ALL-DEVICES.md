# Dark Blue on All Devices

This release removes pale/white marketing backgrounds on desktop, tablet, and phone while preserving the generated customer-site preview's independent theme controls.

- Dark navy gradient applied throughout the Bluvixa marketing site.
- White headings and light-blue body text enforced for mobile readability.
- Ownership, pricing, steps, license, and marketing cards use high-contrast dark glass surfaces.
- Builder, preview navigation, uploads, domains, local drafts, and backend scaffolding remain intact.
- Locked pricing is consistent in both the page and builder logic:
  - Starter: $15/month, $180/year, $499 buyout
  - Professional: $29/month, $348/year, $599 buyout
  - Advanced: $49/month, $588/year, $699 buyout
