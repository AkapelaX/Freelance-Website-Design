Website Buyout License Summary

Customer receives

A perpetual right to use, modify, host, transfer, and commercially operate the purchased exported website package.

Third-party assets

Remain governed by their original licenses. Items that cannot be redistributed are identified or excluded from export.

After export

Self-hosted code is no longer automatically maintained by Bluvixa unless a separate support or hosting service is active.

CUSTOMER POLICIES & SUPPORT

Clear terms before you subscribe or purchase.

These policies explain Bluvixa billing, trials, cancellations, refunds, digital delivery, privacy, and customer support.

REFUND & DISPUTE POLICY

Refund eligibility

  • Annual subscriptions include a 7-day free trial. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the annual charge.
  • After an annual subscription charge is processed, customers may request a refund within 14 calendar days of the charge. Refund requests are reviewed for account usage, duplicate billing, technical failure, fraud, or other relevant circumstances.
  • No refund is guaranteed after substantial use of the builder, hosting, publishing, cloud storage, export tools, or other paid services, except where required by law.
  • Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Bank processing times vary.
  • Customers should contact Bluvixa before filing a payment dispute so we have an opportunity to investigate and resolve the issue.
CANCELLATION POLICY

Cancel without losing clarity

  • Subscriptions can be canceled through the Bluvixa billing portal or by contacting support.
  • Cancellation stops future renewals. Access normally continues until the end of the paid billing period unless otherwise stated.
  • Canceling during the 7-day trial prevents the annual subscription charge.
  • Canceling a subscription does not automatically refund a payment already processed.
  • Customers should export or save any content they need before access ends. Data may be removed after account closure or the applicable retention period.
DIGITAL DELIVERY & FULFILLMENT

How services and website packages are delivered

  • Subscription access is delivered electronically to the customer’s Bluvixa account after successful checkout and account verification.
  • Builder access, hosting tools, project storage, and account features are provided online; no physical product is shipped.
  • A website buyout is a one-time digital purchase for the specific completed website associated with the order.
  • When a buyout payment is confirmed, eligible source files and customer-owned assets are made available through the account export feature or another secure electronic delivery method.
  • Delivery may be delayed if payment review, identity verification, technical remediation, or third-party licensing review is required.
WEBSITE BUYOUTS

Custom digital-product terms

  • Before export or delivery, a buyout refund request may be considered if no downloadable package has been generated or provided.
  • After the source-code package has been generated, downloaded, transferred, or otherwise delivered, the buyout is generally non-refundable because the digital files cannot be returned.
  • This policy does not limit rights that cannot legally be waived.
  • Third-party assets remain subject to their original licenses and may be excluded from the export when redistribution is not permitted.
