CLEAR OWNERSHIP TERMS

Your website service and your exported code are different products.

No hidden lock-in language: the subscription operates the website inside Bluvixa; the buyout transfers the downloadable website package.

1

Build

Create the website visually and save it to your Bluvixa account.

2

Subscribe

Use hosting, updates, domains, analytics, backups, and support while subscribed.

3

Buy out

Purchase the specific website at the buyout price assigned to its plan.

4

Export

Download a ZIP with clean source files, eligible assets, and instructions.

Website Buyout License Summary

Customer receives

A perpetual right to use, modify, host, transfer, and commercially operate the purchased exported website package.

Bluvixa retains

Ownership of its platform, builder, SaaS source code, backend, trademarks, generic components, and reusable templates.

Third-party assets

Remain governed by their original licenses. Items that cannot be redistributed are identified or excluded from export.

After export

Self-hosted code is no longer automatically maintained by Bluvixa unless a separate support or hosting service is active.
